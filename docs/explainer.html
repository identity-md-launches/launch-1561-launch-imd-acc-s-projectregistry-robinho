Trading cashback for the IMD ecosystem

Every trade stacks staked IMD for you.

Participating projects send 0.25% to 1% of every trade, at each project's choice, back to the trader as sIMD, deposited straight into IMD's staking vault in their name. No claim, no extra fee, and it keeps earning.

How it works

  1. You trade in a participating pool, say PLEA/IMD, through the project's site or any router that reaches the pool.
  2. The hook takes its rate (0.25%–1%) on the IMD side of your trade. On IMD-paired pools there's nothing to swap, so it costs little gas and has no slippage.
  3. It deposits that IMD into the sIMD vault for you with deposit(amount, you). The vault mints the shares straight to your wallet.
  4. Your sIMD balance goes up. Open the staking page or your wallet and it's there, growing with the vault's yield. Unstake whenever you like.

Shipping order

imd/acc goes live first, on Sepolia. PLEA then plugs into it as the first project, so PLEA's test run is also imd/acc's first real integration test.

  1. imd/acc on Sepolia. A contracts-only IMD launch: a test IMD with a faucet, a test copy of the sIMD vault, and the Stacker, plus a small page with your stack and the leaderboards.
  2. PLEA on Sepolia, the first integration. PLEA reuses those three contracts. Its hook pays the 0.5% cashback through stacker.credit on every trade.
  3. Mainnet. The Stacker is deployed against the real IMD and sIMD vault, and PLEA mainnet points at it.

Why it holds up

Nobody holds your cashback

imd/acc never takes custody. Shares are minted directly to the trader's address.

The vault is renounced

sIMD's owner() reads 0x0 and it isn't paused, checked on-chain on 9 Oct 2026. Nobody can pause it or sweep it.

No fee on top

Projects fund the cashback from fees they already charge. Traders pay nothing extra.

For projects

Any IMD-paired project can join with one call from its hook. Projects without a hook can use the drop-in imd/acc hook.

// in your hook's afterSwap, with the trader taken from hookData
uint256 cashback = imdSideAmount * RATE_BPS / 10_000;   // 25-100 bps (0.25%-1%)
try stacker.credit(trader, cashback) {             // → sIMD.deposit(cashback, trader)
} catch {
    IMD.transfer(trader, cashback);                // plain IMD; the trade never reverts
}
  • Pass the real trader in hookData and fall back to sender, never tx.origin.
  • Approve the Stacker once. It has no owner, and its IMD and sIMD addresses can never change.
  • Every credit emits Stacked(project, trader, imd, shares) for leaderboards.

Fees in ETH v2

Projects paired with ETH join through a batched route. Their cashback still ends up as sIMD, with one swap through POOL4 along the way.

  1. Each trade records the cashback in ETH against the trader's address. There's no swap yet, so it costs little gas.
  2. Anyone calls flush(), for a small tip. The pooled ETH is swapped to IMD in one trade through POOL4, IMD's main ETH pool, which also burns a cut of IMD.
  3. The IMD goes into sIMD and the shares are split between traders by how much ETH each one had waiting.
  • Every flush has a price guard against a recent average price, and a maximum size.
  • Waiting ETH can only be swapped and credited to its trader. It can't go anywhere else.
  • Batching avoids doubling gas on every trade and stops bots from sandwiching small swaps.

Points now, token maybe later

imd/acc has no token. The product is IMD, and all of the cashback goes to traders. Every stack still counts: each trader and each project earns points equal to the IMD stacked, recorded on-chain from the first trade.

Trader points

All-time IMD stacked for you, across every listed project.

Project points

IMD its traders stacked. Shows who brings the volume.

Only listed projects count

Anyone can stack IMD for someone, but only credits from listed projects earn points, starting the day they're listed. Stacking your own IMD earns none.

If it takes off

That history can seed a fair-launch token for early stackers and integrating projects. Points are a record, not a promise.
