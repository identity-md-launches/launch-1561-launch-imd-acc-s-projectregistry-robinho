A trader with code (a smart-contract wallet, which may not exist at the same address on Ethereum) is paid plain IMD here at once (Paid, no points) unless it has named an Ethereum recipient for itself with setEthereumRecipient (only the wallet itself can; zero clears it); an EIP-7702 delegation (23 bytes starting 0xef0100) is a plain wallet and goes through the queue. flush(recipients) (at most 50; a recipient listed twice or with nothing waiting is skipped) takes from each listed recipient only the whole bridge steps of their waiting IMD (the step read from IMD's decimalConversionRate, 0.000001 IMD; dust below a step stays waiting, untipped, until it adds up to one), pays the caller a tip from that part, 2% per 12 hours waited and at most 2% (tipBps), rounded down to a step, delivers the rest, and sends the total in one IMD.send to the receiver with the compose message abi.encode(recipients, amounts) and executor options the contract builds (lzReceive 250,000 gas; lzCompose 200,000 + 80,000 per recipient), paying the bridge fee from msg.value (quoteFlush gives it; the endpoint refunds the excess to the caller). If the send fails, nothing changes. Nobody can withdraw, redirect or freeze anything; the only way out is to the receiver fixed at deployment. nonReentrant; OpenZeppelin SafeERC20.
